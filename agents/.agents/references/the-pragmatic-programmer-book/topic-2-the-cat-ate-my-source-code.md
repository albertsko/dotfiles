## Topic 2: The Cat Ate My Source Code

Pragmatic programmers take full responsibility for their actions, education, and career. When problems arise, team trust depends on honest communication, accountability, and providing actionable options instead of excuses.

### The Pragmatic Approach

- Accept responsibility for agreed outcomes, risks, and commitments.
- Build team trust through honest communication, reliability, and mutual support.
- Assess risks in advance and prepare contingency plans for potential failures.
- Flush out weak excuses before speaking by evaluating how they sound.
- Offer actionable solutions, alternative options, and resource requests when presenting problems to managers or teammates.

### Common Mistakes

- Making excuses or blaming third-party vendors, languages, management, or team members when failures occur.
- Failing to prepare backup strategies or contingency plans for known risks.
- Hiding errors or technical difficulties out of fear of appearing weak.
- Saying a task cannot be done without exploring alternatives, workarounds, or additional resource needs.
- Damaging team trust by failing to deliver commitments and offering unbelievable justifications.
