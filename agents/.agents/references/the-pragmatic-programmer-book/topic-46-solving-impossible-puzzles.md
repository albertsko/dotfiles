## Topic 46: Solving Impossible Puzzles

Apparent software engineering impasses often stem from false assumptions rather than real technical limitations. When obvious approaches fail, developers must distinguish absolute constraints from preconceived notions to uncover hidden degrees of freedom. Identifying true boundaries expands the solution space and reveals workable answers. Furthermore, stepping away from intractable problems allows the subconscious mind to find solutions, while explaining the issue to others clarifies hidden assumptions.

### The Pragmatic Approach

- Enumerate all potential paths forward without prematurely dismissing any option.
- Challenge every assumed limitation to verify whether a constraint is absolute or merely a preconceived notion.
- Categorize and prioritize constraints, addressing the most restrictive constraints first.
- Step away from the keyboard to let the subconscious mind process complex problems.
- Explain the problem aloud to a colleague to uncover hidden assumptions and clarify root goals.
- Document daily engineering experiences to build a deep foundation of past knowledge for future problem solving.

### Common Mistakes

- Repeating obvious solutions continuously despite ongoing failure.
- Confusing preconceived notions or artificial boundaries with absolute technical constraints.
- Dismissing unconventional solutions without proving why those paths cannot work.
- Forcing conscious effort continuously on an intractable problem instead of taking a break.
- Panicking when encountering complex engineering puzzles instead of evaluating degrees of freedom.
