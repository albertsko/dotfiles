## 2. The Cat Ate My Source Code

Pragmatic programmers take responsibility for their work, learning, careers, commitments, and mistakes, which builds the trust that teams need for creativity and collaboration. Before accepting responsibility, assess risks outside your control and decline situations that are impossible, unacceptably risky, or ethically questionable; after accepting it, expect accountability, admit ignorance or errors honestly, and prepare contingencies. When problems arise, replace blame and excuses with practical options to recover, prevent recurrence, obtain resources, learn what is missing, or ask for help.

### The Pragmatic Approach

- Assess risks, feasibility, and ethical implications before accepting responsibility.
- Honor accepted commitments and expect accountability for their outcomes.
- Admit mistakes and gaps in knowledge honestly, then find the missing answers.
- Anticipate failures outside your control and prepare contingency plans.
- Test excuses before voicing them by considering what others will ask and what else you can try.
- Present concrete recovery and prevention options instead of blaming tools, vendors, managers, or coworkers.
- Ask for the resources, user input, training, or help needed to succeed.
