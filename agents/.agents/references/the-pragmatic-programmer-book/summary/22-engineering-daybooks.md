## 22. Engineering Daybooks

An engineering daybook is a paper notebook for recording work-related details as they arise, including meeting notes, current tasks, debugging values, reminders, sketches, ideas, and doodles. It provides a more reliable record than memory, preserves ideas without disrupting present focus, and creates a reflective pause that can expose mistaken thinking. Writing by hand offers something distinct from typing, and accumulated daybooks also preserve a personal history of projects and people.

### The Pragmatic Approach

- Keep a paper notebook and pen close while you work.
- Record useful details as they arise, including decisions, observations, measurements, reminders, and ideas.
- Capture unrelated ideas immediately, then return your attention to the current task.
- Use the act of writing to slow down, reflect, and challenge what you have just done.
- Label each full daybook with its date range and keep it for future reference.
- Try the practice for one month, then assess whether it improves recall, focus, and reflection.
