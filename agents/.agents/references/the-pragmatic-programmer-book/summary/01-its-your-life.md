## 1. It’s Your Life

Your life and career are yours to direct. Software developers have unusual freedom because their skills are in demand, work across geographic boundaries, support remote work, and earn good pay. When work is boring, underpaid, toxic, or stagnant, exercise your agency: try to improve the situation, keep your skills current, ask for the conditions you want, and find a better opportunity when the current organization will not change.

### The Pragmatic Approach

- Take responsibility for shaping your career instead of waiting for circumstances to improve.
- Try to fix an unsatisfying work environment, but stop after a reasonable effort and move to a better organization.
- Invest personal time in learning interesting new technologies and keeping your skills current.
- Ask for the working conditions you want, including remote work.
- Find an employer that supports your goals when the current one refuses.
