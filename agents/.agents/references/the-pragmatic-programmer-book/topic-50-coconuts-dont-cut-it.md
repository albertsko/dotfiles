## Topic 50: Coconuts Don’t Cut It

Software teams often fall into cargo cult development by imitating superficial processes, ceremonies, or artifacts of successful companies without adopting the underlying context or discipline. Adopting popular tools, titles, or structures does not guarantee success if the practices do not fit your specific environment and goals.

### The Pragmatic Approach

- Adopt practices based on actual effectiveness rather than current trends or brand names.
- Experiment with ideas on small teams, keep effective techniques, and discard unnecessary overhead.
- Tailor methodologies by combining useful technical and project management practices from multiple sources.
- Focus on the primary goal: delivering reliable, working software whenever users need it.
- Build flexible practices that adapt as organizational needs and contexts evolve.

### Common Mistakes

- Copying process frameworks from large tech companies without matching their scale, infrastructure, or operational context.
- Implementing ceremony artifacts in name only while ignoring fundamental engineering principles.
- Rigidly following certification rules instead of evaluating what delivers value.
- Treating a single software methodology as complete and unchangeable.
