## Topic 1: It’s Your Life

Software development offers extraordinary freedom, remote work opportunities, and high demand for skills. You own your career, so take responsibility for changing work situations that no longer serve you.

### The Pragmatic Approach

- Take agency over your professional life instead of waiting for external improvement.
- Invest your own time in learning new technologies when your current role leaves you behind.
- Fix problems in your work environment or move to an organization that aligns with your goals.

### Common Mistakes

- Accepting job dissatisfaction passively while expecting employers to solve it.
- Letting technical skills stagnate while blaming companies for a lack of training.
- Remaining in toxic or boring workplace environments without attempting to change them or leave.
